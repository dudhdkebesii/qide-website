About Us

Founded on 16 August 2007 and headquartered in Jiangmen, Guangdong, Qide was listed on the Shenzhen ChiNext Board on 26 May 2021 (stock code 300995.SZ).

As a national high-tech enterprise and national SRDI "Little Giant" SME, Qide specializes in the R&D, production and sale of eco-friendly and functional modified plastics — mainly modified nylon (PA) and polypropylene (PP) composites, precision injection molds and carbon-fiber composite products.
