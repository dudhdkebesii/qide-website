About Qide

About Us

Founded in 2007 and listed on the Shenzhen ChiNext Board on 26 May 2021 (300995.SZ), Qide is a national high-tech enterprise specializing in eco-friendly and functional modified plastics.

  • Full chain: R&D → precision molds → injection molding → auto parts
  • Revenue mix: modified PA 42.08% · other 29.96% · modified PP 12.62%
  • Certified to ISO9001, ISO14001, IATF16949, GRS4.0, AS9100D
  • Partnerships with BIT, SCUT, Wuyi University
